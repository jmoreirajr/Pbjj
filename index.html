Meet Your Instructor

Joe Moreira Jr.

1st Degree Black Belt under Grandmaster Joe Moreira — a 9th degree red belt, UFC veteran, and pioneer of jiujitsu with an association spanning over 22 countries worldwide.

Joe Jr. began training as a young child and has competed at the highest levels of the sport. He teaches the Moreira Method — a system built over 400 years of ancestry and development, based on rules and principles rather than a memorized series of techniques.

His mission: to make the community a better, more integrated place by introducing an art that connects the mind and body.
