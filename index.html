What Happens At Your First Class

1. Warm Welcome

Arrive 10 minutes early, meet the coach, and get a quick tour of the academy.

2. Easy Warm-Up

Light movement to get your body ready — no experience or gear required.

3. Learn 2–3 Techniques

We'll teach you real BJJ moves you can use, broken down step by step.

4. Light Practice

Practice with a partner at your own pace. Zero pressure to go hard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience to start?

Not at all! Most of our members had zero experience before their first class.

What should I wear to my first class?

Just wear comfortable athletic clothes (like you'd wear to the gym). We'll provide any gear you need.

Is BJJ good for kids?

Absolutely — it builds discipline, confidence, focus, and real self-defense skills in a safe, structured setting.

How much does membership cost?

Pricing depends on the plan — contact us and we'll walk you through the best option for your goals.

Is there a contract?

We offer flexible month-to-month options as well as longer-term plans with discounts.
