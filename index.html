OUR PROGRAMS

Four programs, one community. Whatever your goal, there's a class built for you.

Kids BJJ

Focused on structural development of confidence, discipline, and technical ability — helping kids stay safe and healthy in all aspects of life.

Ages 4–13

Adult BJJ (Gi)

Kimono-based class focused on the fundamentals and principles of jiujitsu — the true heart of our sport. No experience needed. Connect the mind and body and learn to play human chess.

All Levels · No Experience Needed

No-Gi BJJ

Same fundamentals as our Adult BJJ class, without the gi. More dynamic and less technical than the kimono class. No experience needed.

No Age Limit

Women's Self Defense

Street self-defense workshop covering body language awareness and jiujitsu techniques to help keep women safe in all aspects of life. Women only.

No Age Required

MEET YOUR INSTRUCTOR

Joe Moreira Jr.

1st Degree Black Belt under his father, Grandmaster Joe Moreira — a 9th degree red belt, UFC veteran, and pioneer of jiujitsu with an association spanning over 22 countries worldwide.

Joe Jr. started training as a young child and has competed in multiple events at the highest level. He teaches using the Moreira Method — a system built on over 400 years of ancestry and development, based on rules and principles rather than a rigid series of techniques.

His mission is to make the community a better, more integrated place by introducing an art that connects the body, mind, and spirit — training through diversity, building connections that carry beyond the mats, and helping students love themselves and others more.
